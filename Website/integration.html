Integration guide

How integration with aggregators will work

This guide describes how integration will work once our game server is built and the games are certified, and what works today. Full API docs come with the first certified release.

What works today

  • A page per game, in an iframeEach game is an HTML5 page at its own address, such as brainer.games/space-crash/, and runs in an iframe in phone and desktop browsers. English only, with play money in US dollars.
  • Signals to the page around itOn brainer.games the game tells the page around it when it is ready to show, and when the player goes full screen with the game’s own button, so the page can hide its own bars. In a frame on another domain the page gets neither message, and the game plays its own intro.
  • Provably fair maths, publishedOne HMAC-SHA256 roll per step, or per round in Fly High, with a client seed the player can set and a fairness check in every game. RTP up to 97%, bets from $0.10 to $200 and a $10,000 max win. How the check works
  • No game server or wallet yetIn the demos the game in your browser plays the server’s part, and nothing connects to a wallet.

How a real-money round will run

A Brainer game server will hold the seeds and make every roll. The game in the player’s browser will only show the results.

  1. Launch. Your platform opens the game by URL with a session token, the game id, the currency, the language, real or demo mode, and an exit URL back to your lobby.
  2. Bet. When the player starts a round, the game server debits the bet through your wallet API.
  3. Play. The game server makes each roll from its seeds and the player’s client seed, and the game shows the result.
  4. Cash out. When the player cashes out, the game server credits the win through your wallet API.
  5. Safe retries. Every call carries its own transaction id, so a retry never debits or credits twice, and a call that fails is rolled back.

Settings and tools

  • Limits per casino and currencyBet limits and the max win, set per operator and currency.
  • Round historyEvery round with its seeds, for your support team and for disputes.
  • Responsible gaming hooksThe games will take your platform’s reality checks, limits and other player protection.
  • Signals to your pageThe ready and full-screen messages for a lobby on your own domain.
  • Free rounds and promosLater, with the promo tools on our roadmap.

From agreement to go-live

  1. Agreement with the aggregator.
  2. Certification and licence. A test lab certifies the games, and we get the licence each market requires. Neither has started yet.
  3. Staging. We integrate on the aggregator’s staging environment, through their API.
  4. QA. Both teams test the integration.
  5. Go-live, market by market.
